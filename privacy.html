Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026 (polls, alerts, leaderboard and invites added)

NewsTrade shows an economic calendar, market prices, charts and news, and lets members chat, keep a watchlist and keep a trade journal. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how to delete your data.

Using NewsTrade without an account

You can use the calendar, prices, charts and news without signing up. Your time zone, watchlist, starred events and market filter are saved only in your own browser or app on your device. We don't use advertising trackers. To count visits, each device gets a random number stored on that device; we record when the app is opened, which link tag brought you (for example ?src=whatsapp), whether you use the installed app, and your language. This can't identify you, and only the NewsTrade owner sees the totals.

We never see or store your card details. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who helps us run NewsTrade

  • Supabase: accounts, database, chat and comments.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app and the market data files.
  • Dodo Payments: handles Premium payments on the website. They receive the details you enter at checkout.
  • Brevo: sends the email alerts you turn on.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services: deliver the notifications you turn on to your phone.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and code to your device, which means they see your IP address.

Market prices, calendar data and headlines come from public sources. Headlines link to the original publisher, whose own privacy policy applies when you open them.

Public content

Chat messages and comments can be read by anyone using NewsTrade, together with your name. Don't post personal or financial information. Messages are filtered for offensive words, and you can report a message or block a person from the buttons next to it. Nothing in NewsTrade is financial advice.

Delete your account and data

In the app: sign in, tap your name at the top, then tap Delete my account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, settings, trade journal, alerts, poll votes, leaderboard entry, invite record, and all your chat messages and comments straight away. If you have Premium, cancel it first from your Dodo Payments receipt email so you aren't charged again. Payment records that the law requires us to keep are held by Dodo Payments.

Can't sign in? Ask for deletion through the developer contact email on the NewsTrade Google Play page, sending it from the email address on your account. We'll delete your account within 30 days.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account. Market data and headlines are refreshed every hour and old copies are overwritten.

Children

NewsTrade is meant for adults interested in financial markets. It isn't designed for children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
